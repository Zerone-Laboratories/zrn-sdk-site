zrn-frosted-diffusion

What lies behind an element shows through it, blurred and grainy, under a theme tint at 75 %. The element's own text and icons stay sharp on top. App bars, headers and composers are the use.

Zelement_set_frosted_diffusion(bar, Z_FROSTED_DIFFUSION_NORMAL, Z_RES_PHONE_CHROME_BRUSH);
Zset_frosted_diffusion_enabled(false);   // the system-wide switch

A clip of it over scrolling content is on Animations. These pictures were drawn by zrnui's GLES2 renderer, offscreen through Mesa on a laptop, from the uncommitted working tree on top of 71a5ace. The program is gen/frost.c. A bar 150 px tall lies over the top of coloured rows.

Z_FROSTED_DIFFUSION_NONE

NONE

Z_FROSTED_DIFFUSION_NORMAL

NORMAL

Z_FROSTED_DIFFUSION_ASCENDING

ASCENDING: full at the top, fading down

Z_FROSTED_DIFFUSION_DESCENDING

DESCENDING: full at the bottom, fading up

The software renderer does not blur: it fills the element with the tint. When the system-wide switch is off (ztOS Settings, theme, frosted diffusion, or frost = 0 in ~/.config/ztmetro/config), every frosted element paints only its tint, opaque, and nothing is blurred. zrnapp windows follow that switch live. At most 16 elements have the effect at a time.

Checked against zrn-sdk commit 71a5ace (SDK v2). Pictures and clips are drawn by zrnui's GLES2 renderer, offscreen, from code that compiled, against the zrn-sdk working tree on top of that commit (it adds the app bar measured on a Windows Phone 8.1 phone and the Segoe WP font lookup), in Segoe WP.
